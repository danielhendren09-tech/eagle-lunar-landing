JULY 20, 1969 · SEA OF TRANQUILITY

EAGLE

Recreate Neil Armstrong’s lunar landing

The computer just threw a 1202 alarm. The planned site is a boulder field. You have one job: fly Eagle to a clear patch and set her down gently before the fuel gauge hits empty.

Controls

  • Throttle slider — descent engine (this is your most important control)
  • Stick — tilt Eagle so thrust steers you sideways
  • Twist — yaw (turn to face the landing site)
  • Hat (coolie hat on top) — look around. Push it forward to look down on the gold ring. Left/right orbits. The view stays until you hat it back.
  • Trigger — start / continue
  • Thumb button — chase camera or window view
  • Slider — engine, with a hover detent (wider on Jupiter)
  • Stick — tilt · hold 5 to RCS-translate instead
  • Twist — yaw
  • Hat — peek (releases back) · menu D-pad on briefing
  • Trigger — start · hold to abort
  • Thumb — chase / window
  • 3 CUT — engine stop · slider idle clicks it off
  • 4 HLD — AGC attitude hold / PGNCS rates
  • 6 CAM — reset view to chase
  • 7 / 8 — cycle world / mode
  • 9 ABR — abort / fly again
  • 10 / 11 / 12 — mute · invert throttle · invert pitch

Keyboard backup: arrows tilt · Q/E yaw · Shift/Ctrl or 1/2/3 throttle · I/K look down or chase · J/L orbit · C window · R restart
